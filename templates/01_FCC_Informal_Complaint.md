# Template: FCC Informal Consumer Complaint

**Portal:** [FCC Consumer Complaint Center](https://consumercomplaints.fcc.gov)  
**Category:** Internet / Billing  

---

### Description / Narrative to Copy-Paste:

I am filing this formal complaint regarding an improper Early Termination Fee (ETF) of $[INSERT_ETF_AMOUNT, e.g., 110.00] assessed to Comcast/Xfinity Account #[INSERT_ACCOUNT_NUMBER] following cancellation of service due to relocation outside Comcast's service territory.

Comcast claims this fee is assessed pursuant to a 1- or 2-year promotional term contract. However, I have requested and have not been provided an executed contract or an auditable electronic consent record demonstrating contract formation.

Under the federal Electronic Signatures in Global and National Commerce Act (E-SIGN Act, 15 U.S.C. § 7001(d)-(e)), an electronic agreement is legally valid and enforceable only if the record is retained in a form that is capable of being accurately reproduced for later reference by all parties. Internal database markers, billing line items, or unexecuted template documents do not satisfy the statutory requirements of mutual assent or electronic contract retention.

I am fully prepared to pay any legitimate, verified contractual fee immediately upon receipt of an auditable electronic record proving mutual assent. In the absence of such documentation, no enforceable contract exists under federal law.

**Requested Resolution:**
1. Complete cancellation and waiver of the Early Termination Fee.
2. Adjustment of the account ledger to reflect a permanent $0.00 balance in good standing.
3. Written confirmation that no derogatory tradelines will be reported to consumer reporting agencies (Equifax, Experian, TransUnion).
4. Immediate refund of any unearned credit balances or prepaid service funds improperly offset against this unverified fee.
